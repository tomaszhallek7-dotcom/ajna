## 🎵 Music Cues
- **0-5s:** Upbeat tech intro (energy)
- **5-80s:** Calm, professional background (unobtrusive)
- **80-90s:** Energetic tech outro (builds momentum)
- **Sound Effects:** Whoosh (model switch), Ding (links), Keyboard (typing)

## 🎤 Voice Acting Notes
- **Pace:** Natural, conversational (not robotic)
- **Energy:** Start high (0-5s), settle for demo (5-80s), build at end (80-90s)
- **Pauses:** Add 0.5s pause after key points
- **Emphasis:** "Syntezy" - emphasize this word

## 📹 Recording Quality
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (1080p)
- **FPS:** 60fps (smooth animations)
- **Audio:** Clear microphone (no background noise)
- **Lighting:** Well-lit screen + face
